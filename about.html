The Shop

Classic barbering on Little Traverse Bay, Petoskey, Michigan.

Our Story

Up-north pace, big-city sharp

Cuts By The Bay runs on northern Michigan time. That means your appointment isn't a number in a queue — it's a seat in the chair, a good conversation, and a barber who remembers how you like it.

Classic tools, classic techniques, and a shop that feels like Petoskey: warm wood, cold water down the road, and nobody in a hurry.

From a kid's first cut to a hot-towel straight-razor shave, everything on the menu gets the same unhurried attention. That's the whole idea — you leave sharp, and you leave relaxed.

  • Walk-ins welcome, appointments appreciated
  • Kids and first-timers always at ease
  • Hot face shaves by appointment — never rushed
  • Northern Michigan proud — thanks for supporting local
